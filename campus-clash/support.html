Help & support

Campus Clash · Last updated 23 October 2026

Stuck, lost something, or found a bug? We read every message.

Before you write:
  • Bug or crash? Use Settings > Support & Privacy > Report a bug in the game. It sends your game log and system details, which helps us fix it much faster.
  • Problem with another player? Use the report button after the match.
  • Refund? Refunds are handled by Steam: Steam Support.
  • Delete your account or download your data? Both are in Settings > Support & Privacy.

Contact us

Include your player ID (Settings > Support & Privacy > Copy player ID) so we can find your account. We never need your Steam password. Anyone asking for it is not us.
